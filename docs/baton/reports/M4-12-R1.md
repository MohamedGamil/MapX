# M4-12-R1 — Grammar-load bound and work stealing agree under load

Decision D034, option (a): cap new-grammar steals per batch, so that M4-12 AC1 holds. A batch loads at most languages + workers grammars.

## Before

`tests/worker-pool.test.ts`, "deals a real scan of that mix to 4 workers", under a synthetic load (16 `node -e 'for(;;){}'` busy loops on 16 cores, load average 8 to 23):
- first attempt: run 3 of 3 failed with `expected 10 to be less than or equal to 9`. Runs 4 to 8 of that attempt are void, because I edited the source while they ran;
- clean rerun on the untouched source: 9 of 10 passed, and run 5 failed with the same `10 ≤ 9`.

**Cause:** step 4 of `AffinityScheduler.next()` let an idle lane load any grammar once a load paid off, with no count. The plan commits at most lanes + languages − 1 loads, so a second steal of a new grammar crossed the bound. Under load, more lanes go idle early.

## Delivered (`src/core/worker-pool.ts`, `AffinityScheduler`)

- **Budget:** each batch may make languages + lanes grammar loads. "Languages" are the batch's grammar keys, without static files.
- **`held[lane]`** holds what the lane's worker really holds: its grammars from earlier batches, plus every grammar it has been handed (`hand()`). Before, it also counted grammars the plan had only queued for the lane. A free steal (step 3) now takes only grammars the lane really holds.
- **`committed()`** is the loads made so far, plus every grammar still in a lane's own queue that the lane does not hold.
- **Steps:** step 4, a steal of a grammar the lane does not hold, is granted only while `committed() < budget`. Otherwise the lane ends, as D034 allows.
- **Invariant** (stated in the class comment):
  - the plan commits at most lanes + languages − 1;
  - own-queue hands and hands of a grammar the lane holds leave `committed()` unchanged;
  - another lane taking the last queued files of a grammar a lane had not loaded lowers it;
  - only a granted steal raises it, by one.
  
  So the loads made ≤ `committed()` ≤ budget, whatever the order in which lanes ask. A crash is not covered: `resetLane` and `requeue` can exceed the budget, because a replaced worker reloads its grammars.
- `LaneQueue.keysLeft()` supports `committed()`.

## Acceptance criteria

| # | Criterion | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4-worker mix test passes 20 of 20 runs under a stress loop, asserting loads ≤ languages + workers | **Met** | 20 of 20 passed, with 16 busy loops and load 16.6 to 22.8 (log below). The new deterministic tests prove the bound |
| 2 | Bench no more than 10% slower than M4-12 | **Met** | Paired medians: 4,411 ms before, 4,367 ms after (−1%) |

**New tests** (`M4-12-R1: grammar loads stay within languages + lanes whatever the timing (D034)`) drive the scheduler as the pool does: a first file that may load a grammar, then up to 15 files of grammars the lane holds. Lane completion order is fixed and seeded (mulberry32).
- **The 4-lane mix:** 300 orders, with one lane stalled (40× slower), sometimes another racing (4× faster), and jitter of 0.2 to 5× per batch. Each order must hand every file out exactly once, keep `committed()` within the budget after every batch, and load at most 9 grammars.
- **8 lanes:** static files, a warm pool, 6 grammars and uneven sizes, over 200 orders. Loads must stay at most 14.
- **A scripted case:** three lanes, budget 5. Lane 0 takes the fifth load, b. Lane 1 then ends with 28 b files left, while lanes holding b still take them.

**Stress procedure:** the script `M4-12-R1-stress.sh` in the lane scratchpad.
- It starts `nproc` (16) busy-loop node processes with a `trap … EXIT INT TERM` that kills them.
- It waits 5 s, then runs `npx vitest run tests/worker-pool.test.ts -t "deals a real scan of that mix to 4 workers"` 20 times.
- `pgrep -fc 'for\(;;\)'` returned 0 afterwards.
- Result: `passed 20 / 20, failed 0`.

**Bench:** `MAPX_PARSE_WORKERS=8 npx tsx scripts/bench/workers.ts` on the generated 3,000-file fixture. Each run left 12 grammar loads, the index was identical, and there were no hand-backs.

## Limitations

- **Crashes:** a batch with a worker crash can exceed the bound (`resetLane`, `requeue`). This is documented and unchanged.
- **Idle lanes:** D034 lets a lane end while another lane still has files of a grammar it does not hold. The bench shows no cost on the generated fixture.

Changelog: Parallel scans never load more grammars than languages plus workers, however busy the machine (M4-12-R1).
