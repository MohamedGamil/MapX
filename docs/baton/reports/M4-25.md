# M4-25 report: compact terminal logo for init, scan and sync

Owner request 2026-11-02 (D031): scale down the terminal logo of `mapx init` without losing its detail, and show it in `mapx scan` and `mapx sync` too, CLI only.

## Delivered

- **`src/branding.ts`: the compact mark**
  - **Size:** 32 columns × 12 rows, half the 64×23 art each way.
  - **Half blocks:** each cell is split into two (▀ ▄). Each half holds the summed shade level of two horizontally adjacent art characters (0–6 in this art), so all 23 rows survive and only column pairs merge.
  - **24-bit colour:** a half of sum *s* is `#ff02fe` mixed toward the background by `t = 0.2 + 0.8·s/6`. The background is black or white when `COLORFGBG` names a dark or light one (`backgroundOf`), and mid grey (128) otherwise, so the faint glow reads on both.
  - **256 colours:** strongest level first, each level takes the nearest xterm-256 code no stronger level took. The full colour keeps 201, and no two shades merge.
  - **`NO_COLOR`:** shade characters, each the mean of its four art characters.
- **When the mark shows:** only on a TTY of at least 32 columns. Never when `CI` is set (other than `false`/`0`), on `TERM=dumb`, or with `MAPX_NO_BANNER`.
- **`src/cli.ts`:** `printBanner()` runs first in `init` (now the compact mark), `scan` and `update`/`sync`. MCP, `serve` and `workspaces sync` never call it.
- **Rejected alternative:** a 2×2 average into shade characters at the same size. Rendered side by side, it loses the highlight stripe and blurs the inner hole; it remains only as the `NO_COLOR` fallback.
- **Docs and changelog:** `docs/cli-reference.md` (init, scan, update/sync); `CHANGELOG.md` [Unreleased] Added and Changed.

2. **Colour:**
   - known colours for dark, light and unknown;
   - ten `COLORFGBG` forms;
   - 256 colours: as many distinct codes as levels, 201 for the strongest, no 24-bit codes;
   - `NO_COLOR`: the 2×2 mean checked cell by cell, no escape codes; `NO_COLOR=''` stays coloured;
   - every coloured glyph is closed by a reset (no colour bleed).
3. **When it is hidden:** null in a pipe, in CI, on `TERM=dumb`, with `MAPX_NO_BANNER`, and at 31 columns; shown at 32 columns and with `CI=false`.

Visual check: rendered to PNG for dark and light backgrounds with each background setting (`scratchpad`, not in the repository). The ribbon, the highlight stripe and the inner hole stay visible.

## Independent review

A fresh agent got the criteria and the diff, without these conclusions. It found no P0 or P1; all four criteria were met. Its P2s were all fixed before DONE:
- with no `COLORFGBG`, a light terminal showed the faint glow as dark plum: unknown backgrounds now fade to mid grey;
- in 256 colours, levels merged (6 into 4 or 5): each level now has a distinct code;
- colour bleed was untested: it now has a reset test;
- `COLORFGBG` and `NO_COLOR=''` parsing were untested: they now have table tests.

It also noted that CI runners allocating a pseudo-terminal would now print the logo on scan and sync, against the docs' "never in CI". `CI` and `TERM=dumb` now suppress it.

## Limitations

- The background is not queried from the terminal (OSC 11), because that needs a raw stdin round-trip. Unknown backgrounds get the neutral mid-grey shading; known ones get stronger contrast.
- 256-colour codes keep each level distinct, but are coarser than 24-bit colour.
- `NO_COLOR` cannot show half-block shading, so the plain mark is the 2×2 average.
- The owner's own look in their terminal is still the final check.
