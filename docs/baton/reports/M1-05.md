# M1-05 — Laravel-compatible resource parameter names

Date: 2026-10-30. Delivered together with M1-02 … M1-09 as one detector rework (`src/frameworks/detectors/laravel.ts`, the new `laravel-mounts.ts`, and the scanner's route pass). This report covers this task's part.

## Delivered

`resourceParam()` implements Laravel's resource wildcard: `Str::singular` of the last segment, with irregulars (people → person, children → child, …), uncountables, and `-` replaced by `_`. `->parameters([...])`, `->parameter()`, `->only()` and `->except()` are honoured.

## Acceptance

1. Route::resource('blog-categories', …) yields {blog_category}; 'statuses' yields {status}; 'people' yields {person}; ->parameters(['users' => 'admin_user']) yields {admin_user}.
2. The M1-01 scorer shows no regression in any other pattern.

All are met. They are covered by `tests/laravel-mounts.test.ts` (end-to-end scans through the real CLI) and by the M1-01 fixture, now with floors at 100% and exact parameter names.
