# M1-05-R3 — Laravel routes: withoutMiddleware, implicit binding fields on plain routes, repeated chained attributes

## Identification

Milestone: M1; priority: P2; risk: low; estimate min/expected/max: 2/4/6 h; role: PHP/Laravel routing, static analysis.

## Objective

The remaining route registration forms found by M1-05-R2 match Laravel's router: excluded middleware, `{param:field}` in plain route URIs, and repeated RouteRegistrar attributes chained before the verb.

## Dependencies

M1-05-R2 (hard, DONE). Builds on M0-04-R3 (middleware class constants named through the route file's `use` table). No approval gate.

## Scope

- `withoutMiddleware` before the verb (a RouteRegistrar attribute, accumulated), after the verb (`Route::withoutMiddleware`, appended), on groups, and as the `excluded_middleware` key of a group or action array. Class constants are named through the route file's use table, as `->middleware(X::class)` is.
- Resources and singletons: `->withoutMiddleware()`, `->withoutMiddlewareFor()` (replaces per action), the `excluded_middleware` / `excluded_middleware_for` options, and a `Route::withoutMiddleware()` attribute before the resource verb.
- Reported middleware is what `Router::gatherRouteMiddleware` keeps: the route's middleware minus its excluded middleware, both resolved through the Kernel aliases and groups, matched by exact name.
- Implicit binding fields: `{post:slug}` and `{post:slug?}` in a plain route URI or a group prefix are reported as `{post}` / `{post?}` with `metadata.bindingFields`, as `RouteUri::parse` does. A `->prefix()` called after the verb re-parses the URI and drops them, as Laravel does. Resources get the parsed fields of their prefix unless `scoped()` replaces them.
- Repeated chained attributes before the verb or group: `middleware`, `prefix` and `namespace` are replaced (last wins), as RouteRegistrar::attribute does. An action array's `middleware` key replaces a chained middleware attribute. `->middleware()` after the verb still appends.

## Out of scope

- The subclass rule of `Router::resolveMiddleware` (an excluded parent class removes its subclasses): needs class hierarchy resolution; recorded as a limitation.
- Binding fields in domains (`->domain('{account:slug}.x')`).
- `update` as one `PUT|PATCH` route (kept as two by design, D-recorded in M1-05-R1/R2).
- Controller-constructor middleware class constants (M0-04-R3 limitation).

- `tests/fixtures/laravel-routes`: labelled routes for binding fields (strict parameter names).
