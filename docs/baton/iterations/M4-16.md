# M4-16 — Dashboard UX/UI audit with a measured baseline

## Identification

Milestone M4; P1; risk medium; estimate 6/10/16 h. Decision D027.

## Objective

Evaluate the dashboard's look and feel, usability and performance on fixture workspaces, with screenshots and measured baselines, and rank the findings.

## Background

D027. The dashboard (src/ui: main.ts 3,656 lines, styles.css, index.html; 7 tabs; Cytoscape) has had no design review or browser test.

## Dependencies

- M4-15 (hard)

## Out of scope

All implementation: design (M4-17), tooling (M4-19), performance (M4-20), features (M4-21 to M4-23).

## Acceptance criteria

1. scripts/ui/audit.ts serves the dashboard on two fixture workspaces (a generated multi-language project and a copy of MapX's own sources) and captures every tab at 1440x900, 1024x768 and 390x844 (PNG, at most 5 MB in all, under docs/research/dashboard-ux-audit/).
2. The audit records, with the commands that produce them: the dashboard bundle's gzipped size, load time to an interactive graph, graph render time at about 500 and 2,000 nodes, and axe accessibility violations by impact.
3. docs/research/dashboard-ux-audit.md lists the findings per tab, each ranked P1/P2 with a screenshot reference and the task that owns its fix (M4-17, M4-20, M4-21, M4-22 or M4-23).

## Verification

- `npm run check`
