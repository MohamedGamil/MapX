# M4-12-R1 — Grammar-load bound and work stealing agree under load

## Identification

Milestone: M4; priority: P2; risk: low; estimate min/expected/max: 1/3/4 h; role: scheduler / concurrency.

## Objective

A parse batch never loads more grammars than its languages plus its workers (M4-12 AC1), whatever the timing of the workers, so `tests/worker-pool.test.ts` "deals a real scan of that mix to 4 workers" passes deterministically under CPU load. Owner decision D034, option (a): cap new-grammar steals per batch.

## Dependencies

M4-12 (hard, DONE). Decision D034.

## Scope

- `AffinityScheduler` (src/core/worker-pool.ts) keeps a grammar-load budget of languages + lanes per batch and grants a steal of a language the lane does not hold only while the loads already committed stay below it:
  - committed = grammars handed to lanes that did not hold them + grammars still queued in a lane's own queue that the lane does not hold yet;
  - the plan commits at most lanes + languages − 1, so at least one steal is always possible;
  - committed rises only by a granted steal, and drops when another lane takes the last files of a language a lane had not yet loaded (a refund).
- "Holds" for a free steal means the lane's worker really holds the grammar (known before the batch, or handed a file of it), not merely that the plan put it there.
- The invariant is stated in a code comment.
- Tests: a deterministic scheduler test with a controlled completion order that exceeds the bound without the cap; the real 4-worker scan, run 20 times under a synthetic CPU load.

## Out of scope

- Worker replacement after a crash (`resetLane`) and files requeued by a crash: a new worker reloads grammars, so the bound covers batches without crashes. Unchanged.
- Pool sizing (M4-13-R1, held by D035).

## Implementation plan

- `src/core/worker-pool.ts`: `AffinityScheduler` gains `held` (per-lane grammars really held), a `budget`, a `committed()` count, and a check before step 4 of `next()`. `LaneQueue` exposes its languages with files left.
- `tests/worker-pool.test.ts`: a unit test that drives `next()` in an adversarial completion order; the existing real-scan test unchanged in its assertion (≤ 5 + 4).
