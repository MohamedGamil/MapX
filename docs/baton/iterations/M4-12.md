# M4-12 — Language-aware distribution of files to workers

## Identification
Milestone M4; P1; risk medium; estimate 4/8/14 h. Decision D025.

## Objective
Each language gets a set of workers in proportion to its share of the files, and at least one. Small languages are packed onto one worker. A worker whose own queues are empty takes work first from a language it has already loaded, and only then from any other. A worker never idles while files remain.
